OUR PRODUCTS

A World of Authentic Indian Flavors

SARVAMTA Food & Spices Pvt. Ltd. offers a tantalizing array of premium whole, ground & blended spices meticulously crafted to enhance the flavors of your culinary creations. From the fiery warmth of red chilli powder to the aromatic richness of authentic curry blends, each of our products embodies the essence of pure spices sourced directly from trusted regional farms across India.

Whether you’re a seasoned chef, a food manufacturing partner, or an adventurous home cook, our versatile spices & custom blends are designed to inspire creativity and elevate every dish. With an uncompromised commitment to quality, authenticity, and unparalleled taste, SARVAMTA Food & Spices Pvt. Ltd. is your passport to a world of rich Indian flavor.

Our products range from popular ground spices like Pure Turmeric Powder, Red Chilli Powder, Dhana Jeera, Cumin Powder, and Coriander Powder, to a wide variety of blended and whole spices available in consumer packs as well as bulk packaging. We offer these products under our flagship brand “SARVAMTA” as well as full “Private Labeling & Third-Party Contract Manufacturing”.

SPICE CATEGORIES & CONTRACT PACKAGING

WHOLE SPICES

We manufacture and export premium quality whole spices sourced directly from trusted farms across India. Rich in natural essential oils and unadulterated aroma, our whole spices retain 100% natural purity. Our key products include Cumin Seeds (Jeera), Mustard Seeds (Rai), Green Cardamom (Elaichi), Black Pepper, Cloves, and Fennel Seeds.

BLENDED SPICES

Our blended spices are masterfully crafted using traditional Indian recipes and modern hygienic processing to deliver unforgettable flavor. We formulate custom spice blends for home kitchens, restaurants, and white labeling brands. Our popular products include Royal Garam Masala, Tea Masala, Pav Bhaji Masala, Chole Masala, Sambhar Masala, and Biryani Masala.

GROUND SPICES

Finely milled under strict quality control, SARVAMTA ground spices deliver vibrant color, rich texture, and intense flavor to every dish. We offer protective moisture-lock packaging for domestic distribution and global bulk export. Our core products include Pure Turmeric Powder (Haldi), Red Chili Powder (Marchu), Dhana Jeera Powder, and Amchur Powder.

SARVAMTA Food and Spices Pvt. Ltd. is a premier spice manufacturer, exporter, and third-party white labeling specialist bringing pure natural taste & aroma to every kitchen.
