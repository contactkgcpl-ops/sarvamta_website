Welcome To SARVAMTA Food & Spices Pvt. Ltd.

Based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, SARVAMTA Food and Spices Pvt. Ltd. aims to bring pure taste and rich natural spices to every kitchen. Our company started with a strong vision to manufacture fresh, high-quality raw and ground spices for customers across India and global markets.

With 3+ years of experience, SARVAMTA Food and Spices Pvt. Ltd. has become a trusted name in the industry. We are specialists in Spices Manufacturing, Global Export, Contract Manufacturing, Custom Packaging, and White Labeling for leading brands and businesses.

We manufacture a wide range of products including pure Turmeric Powder, Red Chili Powder, Dhana Jeera, Royal Garam Masala, and Custom Seasonings. We provide complete Third-Party Contract Manufacturing and White Labeling services. This means we manufacture and package spices and seasonings under your own brand name with strict quality testing and 100% safety.

Along with domestic manufacturing, we offer Global Spices Bulk Export with protective moisture-lock packaging. At SARVAMTA Food and Spices Pvt. Ltd., we focus on honest quality, affordable pricing, and long-term business partnerships. Thank you for choosing SARVAMTA for your spice manufacturing, contract packaging, and export needs.

WHOLE SPICES

We manufacture and export premium quality whole spices sourced directly from trusted farms across India. Rich in natural essential oils and unadulterated aroma, our whole spices retain 100% natural purity. Our key products include Cumin Seeds (Jeera), Mustard Seeds (Rai), Green Cardamom (Elaichi), Black Pepper, Cloves, and Fennel Seeds.

MAKHANA

ULTRA FINE GRINDING

Our grinding machines grind material at high speed allowing very fine even granulation with perfect texture for variety of spices. This process allows spices to get sprinkled uniformly.

PURE & UNADULTERATED

Our spices and seasonings aren't mingled with or diluted by extraneous matter nor do we indulge in any practices to get more out of a certain quantity of a spice by manipulating it. We provide 100% purity in our products.

NO ARTIFICIAL COLOR ADDED

Our spices spread evenly when sprinkled without the hassle of lump-forming, it gives garnish touch to your food. Also, it results in least wastage of spices.

HYGIENICALLY PACKED

All spices are hygienically packaged with the help of our technically advanced packaging machine. An air-tight packing is done with caution to let it be moisture free. This keeps our spices fresh and bacteria free.
