The story of Top Spices & Seasoning Manufacturers in India - SARVAMTA Food & Spices Pvt. Ltd.

Welcome To SARVAMTA Food & Spices Pvt. Ltd.

Based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, SARVAMTA Food and Spices Pvt. Ltd. aims to bring pure taste and rich natural spices to every kitchen. Founded by Mr. Krina Panchal (Owner), our company started with a strong vision to manufacture fresh, high-quality raw and ground spices for customers across India and global markets.

With 3+ years of experience, SARVAMTA Food and Spices Pvt. Ltd. has become a trusted name in the industry. We are specialists in Spices Manufacturing, Global Export, Contract Manufacturing, Custom Packaging, and White Labeling for leading brands and businesses.

We manufacture a wide range of products including pure Turmeric Powder, Red Chili Powder, Dhana Jeera, Royal Garam Masala, and Custom Seasonings. We provide complete Third-Party Contract Manufacturing and White Labeling services. This means we manufacture and package spices and seasonings under your own brand name with strict quality testing and 100% safety.

Along with domestic manufacturing, we offer Global Spices Bulk Export with protective moisture-lock packaging. At SARVAMTA Food and Spices Pvt. Ltd., we focus on honest quality, affordable pricing, and long-term business partnerships. Thank you for choosing SARVAMTA for your spice manufacturing, contract packaging, and export needs.
